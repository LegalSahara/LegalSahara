HABEAS CORPUS PETITION

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

FACTS

That the detenu, Bilal, was picked up by plain-clothes men from his home in Gulshan-e-Iqbal Karachi.

That the detention occurred three days ago.

That no FIR was registered and no information has been provided about the detenu's whereabouts.

GROUNDS

A. Because the detention of Bilal without any FIR or information about his whereabouts is a clear violation of his fundamental rights as enshrined in the Constitution, particularly Article 9 and 10, and as upheld in C.P.L.A.3637_2019.

B. Because the actions of the plain-clothes men are in contravention of the principles of due process and the rule of law, as emphasized in C.P.L.A.1809_2020 and further reinforced by the judgment in C.P.L.A.3637_2019.

C. Because the failure to provide any information about Bilal's detention or whereabouts constitutes an unlawful detention, which is actionable under the law as per the principles laid down in relevant case law and C.P.L.A.3637_2019.

D. Because the petitioner, Ahmed, has the locus standi to file this petition on behalf of his brother, Bilal, as per the provisions of the law and the principles of natural justice.

E. Because the continued detention of Bilal without any legal basis or justification is a grave violation of his human rights and fundamental freedoms, which this Honorable Court has a duty to protect and uphold, as cited in C.P.L.A.1809_2020.

F. Because it is the duty of the State to ensure that no person is deprived of his liberty without due process of law, and the present case is a clear instance of such deprivation, necessitating the intervention of this Court to uphold the law and the Constitution, as per the precedent set in C.P.L.A.3637_2019.

G. Because Article 9 of the Constitution categorically provides that no person shall be deprived of life or liberty save in accordance with law. The detention herein is mala fide and without lawful authority — a patent violation.

H. Because Article 10 of the Constitution and Section 61 Cr.P.C. mandate that every arrested person be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours. No such production has taken place, rendering the continued detention wholly illegal.

I. Because the arresting officers were plain-clothes personnel whose identity, rank, and legal authority to arrest have not been disclosed — an additional badge of illegality attaching to the impugned detention.

J. Because this Honourable Court, in exercise of its constitutional jurisdiction under Article 199, is not only empowered but duty-bound to protect fundamental rights and issue a writ of Habeas Corpus. Reliance is placed on C.P.L.A.1809_2020. The Respondents must be directed to produce the detenu, submit para-wise comments, and intimate this Court of the place of detention forthwith.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

1. That this Honorable Court may be pleased to issue a writ of Habeas Corpus directing the respondents to produce the detenu, Bilal, before this Court. 
2. That the respondents may be directed to provide information about the whereabouts and the grounds of detention of Bilal. 
3. That the detention of Bilal may be declared unlawful and he may be set at liberty forthwith.

INTERIM/URGENT PRAYER

It is further prayed that in the interim, pending final adjudication:

i. This Honourable Court may be pleased to issue a Rule Nisi calling upon the Respondents to show cause why the detenu should not be released forthwith;
ii. During the pendency of the above rule, the Respondents be directed to produce the detenu before this Honourable Court on the next date of hearing;
iii. The Respondents be directed to intimate this Honourable Court of the place of detention and the identity and rank of the arresting/detaining officers forthwith;
iv. Any other interim order this Honourable Court may deem fit in the interest of justice.

VERIFICATION

I, Ahmed, do hereby solemnly affirm and declare that the contents of this petition are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief, that nothing material has been concealed, and that the petition is filed bona fide.
