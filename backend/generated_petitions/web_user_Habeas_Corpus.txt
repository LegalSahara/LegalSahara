HABEAS CORPUS PETITION

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

FACTS

That the detenu, Hassan, was picked up by unknown persons suspected to be law enforcement agencies from DHA Karachi on April 2, 2026.

That the petitioner, Ayesha Hassan, has visited multiple police stations but no one is acknowledging the detenu's custody.

That the whereabouts of the detenu, Hassan, are currently unknown.

GROUNDS

A. Because the detention of the detenu, Hassan, without disclosing his whereabouts is a violation of his fundamental rights as enshrined in C.P.L.A.3637_2019, which provides that every person has the right to liberty and security of person.

B. Because the law enforcement agencies' failure to acknowledge the detenu's custody is in contravention of the provisions of C.P.L.A.1809_2020, which mandates transparency in such matters and requires the authorities to disclose the whereabouts of the detenu.

C. Because the petitioner, Ayesha Hassan, has the locus standi to file this petition as the sister of the detenu, as provided under the relevant provisions of C.P.L.A.3637_2019, which recognizes the right of family members to seek relief in cases of unlawful detention.

D. Because the detention of the detenu, Hassan, without a valid reason or disclosure of his whereabouts is a clear violation of Article 9 and 10 of the Constitution, as interpreted by the courts in various cases, including those cited in C.P.L.A.3637_2019, which emphasize the importance of protecting individual liberty and ensuring that arrests are made in accordance with law.

E. Because the actions of the law enforcement agencies are in clear disregard of the principles of natural justice and the rule of law, which are fundamental to the administration of justice in Pakistan, as emphasized in C.P.L.A.1809_2020, and which require that all persons be treated fairly and without prejudice.

F. Because this Honorable Court has the jurisdiction to entertain this petition and grant relief to the petitioner under its inherent powers, as recognized by C.P.L.A.3637_2019 and other relevant laws, which empower the Court to issue writs of Habeas Corpus in cases of unlawful detention.

G. Because Article 9 of the Constitution categorically provides that no person shall be deprived of life or liberty save in accordance with law, and the detention of the detenu, Hassan, is a patent violation of this provision, as it is mala fide and without lawful authority.

H. Because Article 10 of the Constitution and Section 61 Cr.P.C. mandate that every arrested person be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours, and the failure to do so in the present case renders the continued detention of the detenu, Hassan, wholly illegal.

I. Because the arresting officers were plain-clothes personnel whose identity, rank, and legal authority to arrest have not been disclosed, which is an additional badge of illegality attaching to the impugned detention, and which further supports the contention that the detention is unlawful.

J. Because this Honourable Court, in exercise of its constitutional jurisdiction under Article 199, is not only empowered but duty-bound to protect fundamental rights and issue a writ of Habeas Corpus, as relied upon in C.P.L.A.1809_2020, and the Respondents must be directed to produce the detenu, submit para-wise comments, and intimate this Court of the place of detention forthwith.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

1. That this Honorable Court may be pleased to issue a writ of Habeas Corpus to the respondents, directing them to produce the detenu, Hassan, before this Court.
2. That this Honorable Court may be pleased to declare the detention of the detenu, Hassan, as illegal and without lawful authority.
3. That this Honorable Court may be pleased to grant such other relief as may be deemed fit in the circumstances of the case.

INTERIM/URGENT PRAYER

It is further prayed that in the interim, pending final adjudication:

i. This Honourable Court may be pleased to issue a Rule Nisi calling upon the Respondents to show cause why the detenu should not be released forthwith;
ii. During the pendency of the above rule, the Respondents be directed to produce the detenu before this Honourable Court on the next date of hearing;
iii. The Respondents be directed to intimate this Honourable Court of the place of detention and the identity and rank of the arresting/detaining officers forthwith;
iv. Any other interim order this Honourable Court may deem fit in the interest of justice.

VERIFICATION

I, Ayesha Hassan, do hereby solemnly affirm and declare that the contents of this petition are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief, that nothing material has been concealed, and that the petition is filed bona fide.
