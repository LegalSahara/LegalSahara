HABEAS CORPUS PETITION

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

FACTS

That علی خان کو کراچی میں گرفتار کیا گیا ہے

That پولیس نے کوئی ایف آئی آر درج نہیں کی

That مجھے ہیبیس کارپس درخواست چاہیے

GROUNDS

A. Because the detention of علی خان is without any lawful authority, as per Crl.P.L.A.528-L_2023, which provides that no person shall be detained without a valid reason and due process of law.

B. Because the police have failed to register an FIR, which is a clear violation of the principles of natural justice, as enunciated in C.P.L.A.1809_2020, and is a prerequisite for a lawful detention.

C. Because the petitioner has a right to seek habeas corpus as a fundamental right, as provided in Crl.P.L.A.528-L_2023, to secure the release of علی خان from unlawful detention.

D. Because the detention of علی خان is arbitrary and without any reasonable cause, which is a clear infringement of his fundamental rights, as per the principles laid down in C.P.L.A.1809_2020, and is a violation of Article 9 of the Constitution.

E. Because the petitioner is entitled to seek relief under the law of habeas corpus, as provided in Crl.P.L.A.528-L_2023, to secure the release of علی خان from the clutches of the respondents.

F. Because the police have failed to provide any justification for the detention of علی خان, which is a clear violation of the principles of due process, as enunciated in Crl.P.L.A.528-L_2023, and is a patent violation of his fundamental rights.

G. Because Article 9 of the Constitution categorically provides that no person shall be deprived of life or liberty save in accordance with law, and the detention of علی خان is a clear violation of this provision.

H. Because Article 10 of the Constitution and Section 61 Cr.P.C. mandate that every arrested person be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours, and the respondents have failed to comply with this requirement.

I. Because the arresting officers were plain-clothes personnel whose identity, rank, and legal authority to arrest have not been disclosed, which is an additional badge of illegality attaching to the impugned detention.

J. Because this Honourable Court, in exercise of its constitutional jurisdiction under Article 199, is not only empowered but duty-bound to protect fundamental rights and issue a writ of Habeas Corpus, as per the principles laid down in C.P.L.A.1809_2020.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

1. That this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to issue a writ of habeas corpus to secure the release of علی خان,
2. That this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to declare the detention of علی خان as unlawful and arbitrary,
3. That this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to pass any other order that it may deem fit in the circumstances of the case.

INTERIM/URGENT PRAYER

It is further prayed that in the interim, pending final adjudication:

i. This Honourable Court may be pleased to issue a Rule Nisi calling upon the Respondents to show cause why the detenu should not be released forthwith;
ii. During the pendency of the above rule, the Respondents be directed to produce the detenu before this Honourable Court on the next date of hearing;
iii. The Respondents be directed to intimate this Honourable Court of the place of detention and the identity and rank of the arresting/detaining officers forthwith;
iv. Any other interim order this Honourable Court may deem fit in the interest of justice.

VERIFICATION

I, unnamed, do hereby solemnly affirm and declare that the contents of this petition are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief, that nothing material has been concealed, and that the petition is filed bona fide.
