HABEAS CORPUS PETITION

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

FACTS

That the detenu, Ali Khan, was arrested without warrant from the petitioner's home in Gulshan-e-Iqbal.

That the arrest was made by plain clothes officers.

That the detenu is currently being held at Gulshan Police Station.

GROUNDS

A. Because the arrest of the detenu without warrant is a violation of his fundamental right to liberty as enshrined in Article 9 of the Constitution of Pakistan 1973, as cited in the PRIMARY CITATION.

B. Because the detenu's right to a fair trial and due process has been compromised, as guaranteed by Article 10A of the Constitution of Pakistan 1973, referenced in the PRIMARY CITATION.

C. Because the Constitution of Pakistan 1973, as SUPPORTING CITATION, emphasizes the importance of adhering to legal procedures for arrests and detentions, which was not followed in this case.

D. Because the absence of a warrant for the arrest renders the detention unlawful and thus, grounds for issuing a writ of Habeas Corpus exist, as per the PRIMARY CITATION.

E. Because the actions of the plain clothes officers, in arresting the detenu without proper identification or legal authority, constitute an abuse of power and a violation of the detenu's rights under the Constitution of Pakistan 1973.

F. Because the continued detention of the detenu at Gulshan Police Station without formal charges or a court order is a clear infringement of his rights as protected by the PRIMARY CITATION and the Constitution of Pakistan 1973.

G. Because Article 9 of the Constitution categorically provides that no person shall be deprived of life or liberty save in accordance with law. The detention herein is mala fide and without lawful authority — a patent violation.

H. Because Article 10 of the Constitution and Section 61 Cr.P.C. mandate that every arrested person be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours. No such production has taken place, rendering the continued detention wholly illegal.

I. Because the arresting officers were plain-clothes personnel whose identity, rank, and legal authority to arrest have not been disclosed — an additional badge of illegality attaching to the impugned detention.

J. Because this Honourable Court, in exercise of its constitutional jurisdiction under Article 199, is not only empowered but duty-bound to protect fundamental rights and issue a writ of Habeas Corpus. Reliance is placed on Constitution of Pakistan 1973. The Respondents must be directed to produce the detenu, submit para-wise comments, and intimate this Court of the place of detention forthwith.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

1. That this Honorable Court may be pleased to issue a writ of Habeas Corpus directing the respondents to produce the detenu, Ali Khan, before this Court. 
2. That the respondents be directed to show cause as to why the detenu, Ali Khan, has been arrested and detained without warrant. 
3. That after hearing the respondents, this Court may be pleased to pass an order for the release of the detenu, Ali Khan, if his detention is found to be unlawful.

INTERIM/URGENT PRAYER

It is further prayed that in the interim, pending final adjudication:

i. This Honourable Court may be pleased to issue a Rule Nisi calling upon the Respondents to show cause why the detenu should not be released forthwith;
ii. During the pendency of the above rule, the Respondents be directed to produce the detenu before this Honourable Court on the next date of hearing;
iii. The Respondents be directed to intimate this Honourable Court of the place of detention and the identity and rank of the arresting/detaining officers forthwith;
iv. Any other interim order this Honourable Court may deem fit in the interest of justice.

VERIFICATION
