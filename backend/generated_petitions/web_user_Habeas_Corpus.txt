HABEAS CORPUS PETITION

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

FACTS

That the detenu Ali Khan was arrested without warrant from our home in Gulshan-e-Iqbal.

That the arrest was made by plain clothes officers.

That the detenu is currently being held at Gulshan Police Station.

GROUNDS

A. Because the arrest without warrant is a violation of fundamental rights as established in J.P.238_2008, which emphasizes the importance of due process.

B. Because the use of plain clothes officers for the arrest is a breach of the principles of transparency and accountability in law enforcement, as highlighted in C.P.L.A.1809_2020.

C. Because the detention of Ali Khan at Gulshan Police Station without a valid warrant or charges is unlawful and contrary to the provisions of J.P.238_2008, which safeguards against arbitrary detention.

D. Because the actions of the arresting officers are in contravention of the principles of natural justice and the right to a fair trial, as protected by the constitution and reiterated in J.P.238_2008.

E. Because the petitioner has a legitimate interest in the welfare and liberty of the detenu, and the arbitrary arrest and detention are causing undue hardship and distress, which is a violation of the rights protected under C.P.L.A.1809_2020.

F. Because the failure to produce the detenu before a competent court within the stipulated time frame is a clear violation of the law and the principles of habeas corpus, as underscored by the judgment in J.P.238_2008.

G. Because Article 9 of the Constitution categorically provides that no person shall be deprived of life or liberty save in accordance with law. The detention herein is mala fide and without lawful authority — a patent violation.

H. Because Article 10 of the Constitution and Section 61 Cr.P.C. mandate that every arrested person be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours. No such production has taken place, rendering the continued detention wholly illegal.

I. Because the arresting officers were plain-clothes personnel whose identity, rank, and legal authority to arrest have not been disclosed — an additional badge of illegality attaching to the impugned detention.

J. Because this Honourable Court, in exercise of its constitutional jurisdiction under Article 199, is not only empowered but duty-bound to protect fundamental rights and issue a writ of Habeas Corpus. Reliance is placed on C.P.L.A.1809_2020. The Respondents must be directed to produce the detenu, submit para-wise comments, and intimate this Court of the place of detention forthwith.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

1. That this Honorable Court may be pleased to issue a writ of Habeas Corpus directing the respondents to produce the detenu Ali Khan before this Court. 
2. That the respondents be directed to show cause as to why the detention of the detenu is not unlawful. 
3. That the detenu Ali Khan be released from detention if the same is found to be unlawful.

INTERIM/URGENT PRAYER

It is further prayed that in the interim, pending final adjudication:

i. This Honourable Court may be pleased to issue a Rule Nisi calling upon the Respondents to show cause why the detenu should not be released forthwith;
ii. During the pendency of the above rule, the Respondents be directed to produce the detenu before this Honourable Court on the next date of hearing;
iii. The Respondents be directed to intimate this Honourable Court of the place of detention and the identity and rank of the arresting/detaining officers forthwith;
iv. Any other interim order this Honourable Court may deem fit in the interest of justice.

VERIFICATION

I, Rafsha Rahim, do hereby solemnly affirm and declare that the contents of this petition are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief, that nothing material has been concealed, and that the petition is filed bona fide.
