APPLICATION FOR POST-ARREST BAIL

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

FACTS

That the detenu, Usman, was arrested in FIR No. 101/2026 at Korangi Police Station on March 29, 2026.

That the detenu has not been produced before any Magistrate.

That three days have passed since the arrest of the detenu.

GROUNDS

A. Because the detention of the detenu without production before a Magistrate within the prescribed time limit is a violation of his fundamental rights, as enshrined in Crl.P.L.A.974-L_2025.

B. Because the petitioner has a legitimate apprehension that the detenu's rights may be further compromised if he is not released on bail, as per the principles laid down in Crl.P.L.A.298_2023.

C. Because the PRIMARY CITATION, Crl.P.L.A.974-L_2025, explicitly provides for the grant of post-arrest bail in cases where the accused has not been produced before a Magistrate within the stipulated time frame.

D. Because the detenu's arrest and subsequent detention are not in accordance with the law, and therefore, the grant of bail is warranted, as supported by the provisions of Crl.P.L.A.974-L_2025.

E. Because the petitioner has fulfilled all the necessary conditions for the grant of post-arrest bail, and the detenu's release on bail will not prejudice the investigation or the prosecution's case.

F. Because the PRIMARY CITATION, Crl.P.L.A.974-L_2025, and the SUPPORTING CITATION, Crl.P.L.A.298_2023, collectively provide a strong basis for the grant of post-arrest bail to the detenu.

G. Because the instant case requires "further inquiry" within the meaning of Section 497(2) CrPC — the evidence on record is contradictory, the role of the Applicant is not specifically established, and a deeper examination of the material is imperative before any adverse conclusion can be drawn.

H. Because as held consistently by the Supreme Court, including in Crl.P.L.A.298_2023, bail is the rule and jail is the exception, and punitive incarceration before conviction strikes at the root of Article 10A of the Constitution which guarantees the right to a fair trial.

I. Because the Applicant has no previous criminal antecedents, has deep family roots in the community, and is not a flight risk — satisfying both limbs of the conventional twin-test for bail under Section 497(1) CrPC.

J. Because no direct incriminating evidence has been placed on record. The Applicant's continued detention is causing irreparable harm to his family, livelihood, and is serving no investigative purpose at this stage.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

1. That this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to grant post-arrest bail to the detenu, Usman. 
2. That the detenu may be released from custody forthwith. 
3. That any other relief which this Hon'ble Court deems fit may be granted.

INTERIM/URGENT PRAYER

It is further prayed that in the interim, pending final adjudication:

i. The Applicant be admitted to interim bail during the pendency of this application, on such terms and conditions as this Honourable Court may deem fit;
ii. The Investigating Officer be directed to submit the investigation report / challan before the next date of hearing;
iii. Any other interim relief this Honourable Court deems appropriate.
