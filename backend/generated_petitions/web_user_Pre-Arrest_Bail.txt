APPLICATION FOR PRE-ARREST BAIL
[Extraordinary Relief — Concession Not Right]

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

FACTS

That the petitioner is a respected businessman in DHA Phase 6, Karachi.

That a fake FIR was filed against the petitioner under Section 489-F of the PPC at Darakhshan Police Station.

That the petitioner never received any cheque from the complainant and the signatures on the cheque are forged.

GROUNDS

A. Because the filing of a fake FIR is a clear abuse of the process of law, as held in Crl.P.L.A.645-L_2025, which establishes that the registration of a false FIR is a patent violation of the law and the petitioner's fundamental rights.

B. Because the petitioner has a fundamental right to liberty, as enshrined in Article 9 of the Constitution, which is being threatened by the police's actions, and it is the duty of this Honourable Court to protect the same.

C. Because the police are acting malafide and with ulterior motives, as evident from their threat to arrest the petitioner, which is not supported by any evidence, as per the principles laid down in Crl.P.L.A.1075-L_2020, which emphasizes the importance of protecting citizens from arbitrary arrests.

D. Because the petitioner has a strong prima facie case that the cheque is forged and he is innocent, which is a valid ground for grant of pre-arrest bail, as per Crl.P.L.A.645-L_2025, which highlights the need to consider the petitioner's innocence and the lack of evidence against him.

E. Because the grant of pre-arrest bail is necessary to protect the petitioner from harassment and humiliation at the hands of the police, which is a well-established principle of law, and this Honourable Court has the power to grant such relief.

F. Because the petitioner is willing to cooperate with the investigation and is not a flight risk, which are important considerations for the grant of pre-arrest bail, as per relevant case law, and the petitioner is ready to provide any necessary undertaking to this effect.

G. Because the FIR was registered with an inordinate and unexplained delay, which in itself is a strong inference of mala fide intent and an afterthought designed to harass, coerce, and humiliate the Applicant in a dispute that is civil and personal in nature.

H. Because pre-arrest bail is an extraordinary concession — not a right — and is warranted where, as here, the complainant has an ulterior motive, the FIR is an abuse of the criminal process, and the Applicant is willing to fully cooperate with investigation, as held in Crl.P.L.A.645-L_2025.

I. Because arrest at this stage would cause irreparable reputational harm, financial loss, and emotional trauma to the Applicant and his family, with no corresponding benefit to the investigation whatsoever, and this Honourable Court should exercise its discretion to grant pre-arrest bail.

J. Because as held in Crl.P.L.A.1075-L_2020, this Honourable Court grants pre-arrest bail where the risk of abuse of process and misuse of criminal law in a personal or commercial dispute is apparent on the face of the record, and the present case falls within this category.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

1. That this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to grant pre-arrest bail to the petitioner.
2. That the police be restrained from arresting the petitioner till the disposal of this petition.
3. That any other relief which this Hon'ble Court deems fit may be granted to the petitioner.

INTERIM/URGENT PRAYER

It is further prayed that in the interim, pending final adjudication:

i. The Applicant be granted interim pre-arrest bail forthwith during the pendency of this application;
ii. The Respondents be restrained from arresting the Applicant during the pendency of this application;
iii. Subject to grant of bail, the Applicant undertakes to join investigation whenever called upon and shall not leave the country without prior permission of this Honourable Court;
iv. Any other interim order this Honourable Court may deem fit.

VERIFICATION

I, Mehmood, do hereby solemnly affirm and declare that the contents of this petition are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief, that nothing material has been concealed, and that the petition is filed bona fide.
