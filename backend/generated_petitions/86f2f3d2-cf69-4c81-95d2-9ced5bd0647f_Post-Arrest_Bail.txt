APPLICATION FOR POST-ARREST BAIL

Most Respectfully Sheweth:

FACTS

That the detenu, Saad, was arrested under Section 302 PPC.

That the arrest was made in connection with FIR No. 77/2026 at Malir Police Station.

That the petitioner is the sister of the detenu.

GROUNDS

A. Because the arrest of the detenu was made without sufficient evidence, as per the principles laid down in Crl.P.L.A.725_2023, which emphasizes the importance of a thorough investigation before making an arrest.

B. Because the investigation is still in its preliminary stages and the detenu's presence is not required for the purpose of investigation, as provided in Crl.P.L.A.298_2023, which highlights the need for a nuanced approach to bail in cases where the investigation is ongoing.

C. Because the detenu has a fundamental right to liberty, as enshrined in the Constitution, and his detention is not justified in the present circumstances, as held in Crl.P.L.A.725_2023, which underscores the importance of upholding the constitutional rights of the accused.

D. Because the prosecution has failed to show that the detenu is likely to abscond or tamper with evidence, which is a necessary condition for refusing bail, as per the relevant provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure, and as emphasized in Crl.P.L.A.725_2023.

E. Because the detenu is ready to cooperate with the investigation and abide by any conditions that the court may impose, as per the principles of bail laid down in relevant case law, including Crl.P.L.A.298_2023.

F. Because the continued detention of the detenu would cause him irreparable harm and prejudice, and the interests of justice would be served by granting him bail, as per the guidelines laid down in Crl.P.L.A.725_2023 and Crl.P.L.A.298_2023.

G. Because the instant case requires "further inquiry" within the meaning of Section 497(2) CrPC — the evidence on record is contradictory, the role of the Applicant is not specifically established, and a deeper examination of the material is imperative before any adverse conclusion can be drawn.

H. Because as held consistently by the Supreme Court, including in Crl.P.L.A.298_2023, bail is the rule and jail is the exception, and punitive incarceration before conviction strikes at the root of Article 10A of the Constitution which guarantees the right to a fair trial.

I. Because the Applicant has no previous criminal antecedents, has deep family roots in the community, and is not a flight risk — satisfying both limbs of the conventional twin-test for bail under Section 497(1) CrPC.

J. Because no direct incriminating evidence has been placed on record, and the Applicant's continued detention is causing irreparable harm to his family, livelihood, and is serving no investigative purpose at this stage.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Honourable Court may be pleased to:

1. That this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to grant post-arrest bail to the detenu, Saad.
2. That the detenu may be released on bail on such terms and conditions as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit.
3. That any other relief which this Hon'ble Court may deem fit in the circumstances of the case may be granted.

INTERIM/URGENT PRAYER

It is further prayed that in the interim, pending final adjudication:

i. The Applicant be admitted to interim bail during the pendency of this application, on such terms and conditions as this Honourable Court may deem fit;
ii. The Investigating Officer be directed to submit the investigation report / challan before the next date of hearing;
iii. Any other interim relief this Honourable Court deems appropriate.

VERIFICATION
